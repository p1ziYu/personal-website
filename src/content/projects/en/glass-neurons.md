GLASS NEURONS — Documenting the seismic debate that captivated the AI interpretability world in mid-October 2026: Nick Cammarata, interpretability researcher at Goodfire and formerly OpenAI, published a comprehensive 26,000-word essay titled **"Neural Networks Are Already Clean"**.

Its central contention challenges a decade of machine learning dogma: AI's black box was never fundamentally incomprehensible — we simply were not looking at it properly.

### From 1.7% to 90%: The Metric Transformation

- **The Old Verdict (2023)**: An automated interpretability project at OpenAI (initiated by Cammarata himself) used GPT-4 to explain GPT-2 neurons and could explain only **~1.7%** under its most generous criteria. The consensus hardened that neurons are hopelessly tangled ("polysemantic") and require complex Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs) to decompose.
- **The New Numbers (2026)**: In preliminary audits of Alibaba's open-source **Qwen3-32B** model (conducted largely by AI agents), Goodfire estimates that **roughly 90% of neuron poles have ONE primary job** (often with small related offshoots); **~7% have two related jobs**; and **only ~2-4% are not yet understood**. Cammarata frames this as a working hypothesis.
- **The "Tangled" V-Neuron Re-read**: In InceptionV1, one neuron famously fired for both cat faces and car fronts — cited as evidence for superposition. Cammarata re-read it: it is a detector for sharp **V shapes**. Fox snouts and flames turn it ON; round shapes (like the letter O, donuts, wheels, and full moons) push its activation NEGATIVE (inhibit). Cats and cars both feature sharp V vertices — the neuron had one coherent geometric job all along.
- **Architecture Matters**: Cammarata retrained a modern version of the old architecture at the same size on the same images and observed that **99-100% of final-layer neurons were easy to understand** across a 300× model size range. Much of the field's past frustration was an artifact of dated training design and weak inspection tools.

### What Is "Recursive Generalization"?

Why do some neurons still wear multiple hats? Cammarata argues that neurons grow like branches sprouting related side-shoots. For instance, a language model neuron detecting the token fragment `"iy"` naturally extended to cover `"diy"`, and eventually sprouted to activate on the full phrase `"do it yourself"` — which contains **no letters "i" or "y" at all**. The extra jobs are closely related, demonstrating how the network constructs hierarchical abstractions rather than hiding them.

### Reality Check & Scientific Honesty

1. **Preliminary Audit Estimate $\neq$ Proven Law**: The 90% figure is a preliminary audit estimate, not a proven physical law; Cammarata frames the claim as a working hypothesis (its fourth step is labeled "highly speculative" by Cammarata himself).
2. **No Outside Reproduction Yet**: As of mid-October 2026, no outside researchers have reproduced the audits; the technical companion paper remains unreleased.
3. **Top Researchers Remain Deeply Split**: Google DeepMind's Neel Nanda still believes superposition is real; the research community is split on whether this represents a fundamental turning point.
4. **Commercial Interest**: Goodfire sells tools for looking inside AI — it has a commercial interest in this clean-box narrative being true.
5. **Predicting Consequential Behavior**: Researchers still need to demonstrate that neuron-level explanations can predict consequential behavior in advanced models under real-world pressure.
6. **Hand-Coded Synthetic Demos**: All toy neurons and visual grids on this page are hand-coded synthetic demonstrations illustrating theoretical concepts — they are NOT real Qwen3-32B weights or real model data.
