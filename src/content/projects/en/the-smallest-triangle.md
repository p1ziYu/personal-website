Distribute n points within a unit square such that the smallest triangle formed by any three points has the maximum possible area. Proposed by Hans Heilbronn in 1950, this extremal geometry puzzle has captivated mathematicians for decades.

This playground brings the conjecture to life with live multi-point dragging, exact combinatorial area computation, and an in-browser simulated annealing optimizer. Watch point clouds self-organize across rugged, non-convex mathematical landscapes and discover the beauty of extremal geometry.
