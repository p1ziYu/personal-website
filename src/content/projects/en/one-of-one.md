As third-party cookies face phaseout across modern browsers, passive fingerprinting has emerged as the definitive successor for cross-site tracking. By silently inspecting subtle Canvas 2D anti-aliasing variations, WebGL GPU pipelines, OfflineAudioContext dynamics compressor float curves, screen geometry, and canvas-measured font metrics, trackers assemble a high-entropy identifier in milliseconds without requesting a single permission.

One of One runs entirely inside your local browser without transmitting any data over the network. Explore your device's unique hardware and software signals, examine the cumulative bits of entropy, and discover why common privacy defenses can paradoxically make you easier to identify.
