Emberhold Siege is a full-fledged in-browser tactical tower defense game. Three treacherous paths converge upon the last sanctuary. Strategically position Arrow Towers, Cannons, Frost Spires, Snipers, and Gold Mines to protect the central hearth across fifteen escalating waves. Every tower upgrades through three tiers; enemy hordes feature sprinters, juggernauts, clerics, and shielded sentinels—crowned by titanic Ember Lords every five waves.

Features touch controls, pause, 3x speed, local records, and procedural audio synthesis. Rendered entirely on a local canvas with zero external assets.
