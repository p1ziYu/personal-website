One Beat · The Robotic Time Crystal: Exploring wave coarsening and emergent time crystallization in mechatronic metamaterials. On October 14, 2026, Nature published landmark experimental work led by Corentin Coulais's group at the University of Amsterdam and international collaborators, demonstrating an autonomous macroscopic time crystal constructed from an array of 3D-printed active robotic cells.

While conventional time crystals remain confined to microscopic quantum spins or trapped ions at cryogenic temperatures, this experiment achieves temporal symmetry breaking in macroscopic mechanical systems at room temperature. By engineering nonreciprocal forces between neighbouring units (A's push on B does not equal B's push on A), high-frequency spatial ripples undergo nonlinear wave coarsening, progressively coalescing into longer wavelengths until a 37-unit honeycomb lattice and a 24-unit motorized ring pulse in a single, robust collective cadence resilient against noise and material scaling.
