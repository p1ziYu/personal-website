Physicists at the Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology (OIST) demonstrated the first classical mechanical movement of a centimeter-scale object driven by quantum electron spins. By pairing laser-controlled nitrogen-vacancy centers in diamond with diamagnetic graphite levitation, the team pushed a 128-milligram assembly across roughly 100 nanometers—leaping 8 to 9 orders of magnitude beyond prior spin-mechanics limits toward macroscopic quantum superposition.
