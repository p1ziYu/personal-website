# 痞子宇的个人空间首页插画

`coast-desktop.webp`、`coast-mobile.webp` 和 `avatar.webp` 来自同一张生成的原创插画，使用 Pillow 裁切与压缩。用于本项目的 Firefly 首屏与侧栏头像。

生成提示词：

> Full-bleed desktop hero wallpaper for an Astro personal blog. Rich natural greens and ocean blues, cinematic depth, finely painted foliage. Keep the middle third visually calm for a large white Chinese site title overlay. No person, vehicle, characters, signs, buildings, logos, watermarks, or text. Do not reproduce any existing artwork or named artist's style.
